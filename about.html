Built around one idea: a plan you can keep

onefitness started in a single room with two coaches and a whiteboard. The programs got better every time someone told us what they could not stick to. That is still how we work.

Our story

Most plans fail for reasons that have nothing to do with effort

People arrive motivated. What breaks them is a plan written for someone with more time, better equipment and no other responsibilities. So we stopped writing those.

Every onefitness program ships in two variants, home and full gym, and adjusts its load from the sessions you actually complete rather than the ones the calendar hoped for. Nutrition works the same way: targets that move with your training week instead of a fixed number you learn to ignore.

What we hold to

Four rules that decide every program we write.

  1. 01

    Consistency over intensity

    A session you finish beats a session you admire. We build the week you can repeat, then add load once repeating it is boring.

  2. 02

    Evidence, not folklore

    If a method cannot be explained without a myth about metabolism, it does not go in your plan. We would rather say we do not know.

  3. 03

    Food you recognise

    Vegan, non-vegan and allergy-friendly tracks built from ordinary ingredients, because a diet that needs a specialist shop ends in week three.

  4. 04

    Your data stays yours

    Export every plan and logged session whenever you want, including on the way out. Cancelling takes one click and no phone call.

How a plan gets made

Four steps, about a week from first message to first session.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Goals, training history, injuries, the equipment you actually have and the hours you actually own.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    A movement screen and a baseline session, in the studio or on video, so the starting load is a measurement rather than a guess.

  3. 03

    Your week

    A training block and a matching meal track land in your account, each with a home and a full-gym variant.

  4. 04

    Review

    Every fourth week we read what you completed, not what was scheduled, and rewrite the block around it.
