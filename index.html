Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first session.

How are programs personalised?

You share your goals, experience and available equipment. onefitness builds a week-by-week plan and adjusts the load as your performance data comes in.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Every program ships in a home variant using bodyweight and a single pair of dumbbells, plus a full-gym variant when you have access.

Is meal guidance included?

Yes. Vegan, non-vegan and allergy-friendly tracks are included, with calorie targets that follow your training load.

Can I cancel anytime?

Anytime, in one click. Your plans and logged sessions stay exportable after you leave.

Tell us your goal and we will send a tailored first week — free.
